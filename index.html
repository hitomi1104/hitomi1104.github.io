Professional Bio

I'm an AI and software engineer working on systems integration for a federal healthcare regulatory program, where I connect enterprise platforms (Adobe Experience Manager, Oracle Database, and MuleSoft APIs) into reliable, compliant data flows. Before that, I worked on implementation and machine learning prototyping at two startups, including a Y Combinator–backed company, where I built demand-forecasting models with ARIMA, SARIMA, LSTM, and XGBoost.

I hold a bachelor's degree in mathematics from the University of California, Irvine, and I'm pursuing an M.S. in Computer Information Systems with an AI specialization at Indiana Wesleyan University. I also tutor math, which taught me to explain technical ideas to non-technical people. I work in English and Japanese, and I'm growing toward forward-deployed engineering: working directly with users and customers to turn AI into dependable, working systems.

Personal Value Proposition

I help organizations move AI from a promising demo to a system people trust and use. With a mathematics foundation, hands-on integration experience in a regulated environment, and a habit of testing with real users, I bridge technical teams and the people they serve.
